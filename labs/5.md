Prerequisites

From the Main Dashboard, there are two places to get to the Restore Point Explorers; either the left-hand navigation or scrolling down on the dashboard itself.
Flex Restoring Microsoft Outlook data
Info: Select the Outlook explorer to start with and we will recover some emails as well as downloading a mailbox as a PST file.
Let us first go over some of the sections of explorer to better understand the selections:
(1) Restore Point Date Selector (based on Backup Policy Scopes)(2) Looks for permanently deleted items (slows performance)(3) Restore multiple mailboxes by selecting the organization then selecting multiple mailbox checkboxes from the right-hand side of the page(4) Browsing Mailboxes/Objects individually
Let us start off our restore journey by restoring an entire Mailbox for "OD.HOL.USER.1” 
Find and select our user from the object selection then select “Restore Selected Mailbox” to head into the restore wizard.  
There are 2 different restore options available to us here:-First is the option for an “Express Full Restore”. This option will recover the entire object back to the original location with lightning-fast speeds of 1 to 3 TB per hour. (Yes, Terabytes per hour!) Express is limited to entire object recovery and does not offer granular recoveries at this time. You can start the Express Restore simply by selecting the blue option in the bottom right-hand corner for “Express Full Restore”.-Second recovery option is “Flex Restore”. This option gives you the literal flexibility to recover the object back to original location, to a new alternate location such as a different folder or mailbox or even downloading the object to the browser as a PST or even zip files for OneDrive/SharePoint/Teams recoveries. There are even advanced options to allow for customizing the recovery further.
Feel free to make your own choices on selections then finalize the restore by selecting the option to in the bottom right-hand corner of the restore wizard. 
The process begins immediately in the background and progress can be seen under the notifications section.
*Any restore chosen for “Download in Background (for large objects)” will also have a blue hyperlink when ready to download after the restore is finished under the notifications feature. 
While that restore is running (or it is finished already), let us continue with another type of Outlook restore with the option to Download as a PST! Select the same (or even a different) mailbox and select the Flex Restore radial under the “Restore Selected Mailbox” option, but this time select “Local Computer (download)” to download the PST directly to the lab virtual machine browser.
With the flexibility of flex backups, we don't have to restore the entire mailbox. We can navigate through the mailbox folders, restore individual emails, and even preview their content before deciding to restore them. The "previewing emails" feature is important to highlight because it is not only a powerful feature, but could be a security risk depending on who is previewing the emails and why. We have created an audit event, a notification option, and even role-based access control settings just for this scenario to help secure your platform. 
Mailboxes, folders, and emails can all be recovered to another mailbox (even a specific folder in that target mailbox) or just another folder within the original mailbox.

Adding a new user role
Info: Navigate into the settings menu on the left-side navigation bar.
The “Roles” tab should automatically be selected here and the first thing that you will see is that the Administrator role is automatically generated during onboarding. This role has radials already selected for all the permissions available in the environment, this includes “Preview Emails” which can be turned off even for administrators. The Self-Service role may also be generated from enabling the self-service feature under the system tab of settings.
Let us create a new role. Select “Add new role” in the top right-hand corner to open the new role wizard. We can either select “create from a template” or create your own role from scratch. By default, feel free to use the “Limited Restore” role template. This template is set to restrict the user or group assigned to only being able to log in and restore Outlook or OneDrive accounts for the entire tenant. Without adding additional customizations, this role can only restore objects back to the original location. It is set so that the assigned groups cannot restore to alternate locations or even download any of the files to their workstations. You will also notice that this role has disabled the option to “Preview Emails.” This means that users assigned this role cannot read any email bodies in the explorers.
Select “Create from a template” and under the dropdown, we will select “Limited Restore”.
 
Customize this role further by giving it a new name and additional permissions. (Feel free to change the name to desired, but keep it professional please..)
Modify the role from “Access User Backups for Entire Tenancy” radial to exclude some groups that we want to keep out from this role’s purview. Turn off the setting first to change to “......all except selected groups”.
   
 The overall goal here is to assign this role restore access to every users Outlook and OneDrive EXCEPT the ”IT Help Desk” group which we can add with the “Select Groups” option.
 Once the selections are done, select "Ok" to save the new role.

Self-Service implementation
Info: The last consideration for RBAC is that there is an easy self-service option that you can enable. This option allows anyone in your Microsoft 365 Organization to log into Veeam Data Cloud platform and perform restores for their own Outlook or OneDrive data. They cannot see dashboard data, jobs, etc. and can only interact with their own data, unless the “self-service” role is modified to allow additional capabilities.Here is how you would enable this setting in your VDC UI. Navigate from Settings > System > Access tab then enable the radial and select “save”.
If you would like to see the self-service option, ensure that option is enabled then attempt to log in as a user from the list that does not have a custom role assigned. VDC M365 solution options
There are 2 Veeam Data Cloud for Microsoft 365 product offerings: FLEX and EXPRESS. 
You can have both of these plans under a single PREMIUM plan. 
You do not have to choose to protect your environment with 100% Flex, Express, or Premium and can mix/match plan types.
For instance, you may want to protect the top 20% of your most important user base with Premium and the rest on just the Flex plan.
Here are the main differences between the plans:
FLEX prioritizes control and flexibility to meet your Business Continuity needs. 
Supports Microsoft Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive for Business and Microsoft Teams. 
Offers comprehensive control and customization of backups and retention periods. 
Granular and bulk data recovery options. Data is stored separately from your Microsoft 365 tenant in the Azure location of your choice. 
Offers advanced security capabilities including granular Role Based Access Control.
EXPRESS prioritizes speed and scale to satisfy your Disaster Recovery plans. 
Powered by Veeam’s integration with Microsoft 365 Backup Storage. 
Supports Microsoft Exchange, SharePoint and OneDrive for Business. 
Fast backup of initial Microsoft 365 tenant and other large datasets. Offers bulk restores at scale.
